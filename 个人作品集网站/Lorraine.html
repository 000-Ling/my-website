A little about me

关于我

I notice small things, ask too many questions, and turn fuzzy ideas into something you can hold.

What I believe · 01 Stay open. 看到不懂的东西先试一下，再决定要不要喜欢。新工具不是威胁，是新玩具。
What I believe · 02 Make it real. 想法只有在能被摸到的那一刻才算数。图纸、模型、一条视频，都算。
What I believe · 03 Notice the small. 差 10% 的地方，通常就是全部的差别。90% 合格率不是及格线。

I'm Lorraine — Luo Ling, if you're reading the Chinese side — a final-year AI student in Guilin who keeps ending up in the gap between machines that see and stories that move people.

Officially: machine vision, Python, Java, and how cars learn to read a road. Lately also: AI products — the small, unglamorous tools that take a boring hour out of someone's day. Unofficially: the same question, again and again — why did the model fail on this photo, and is the answer in the data or in my patience?

I like work that leaves a mark. Three thousand audio clips labelled by hand, ninety percent of them clean — not because I hit the pass mark, but because I couldn't stand shipping something half-checked. Two hundred short videos for a high school nobody asked me to archive, and a filing system that quietly made everyone thirty percent faster.

I'm quietly restless. I'd rather build one small, slightly odd thing than describe a big, perfect one. I read in two languages, greet people in three, photograph food the way other people collect stamps. And I care, more than I admit, about how things look — a good palette, a good outfit, a good page are the same craft.

This is a pocket of the internet. The light's on.

我叫罗铃，也可以叫我 Lorraine。桂林人，人工智能专业大四。我的位置一直有点窄：一半在让机器看见，一半在让人愿意看。

正经地说，我学机器视觉、Python、Java，也学车怎么学会看路。最近也在做 AI 产品——那种不起眼的小工具，能把别人一天里无聊的一小时拿掉。不太正经地说，我老盯着同一张图问：为什么它偏偏在这张图上翻车？是数据的问题，还是我不够耐心？

我喜欢做完的、看得见的东西。三千条音频，一条条听、一条条标，合格率 90%——不是及格线到了就交差，是我自己接受不了"差不多就行"。两百条校园短视频，没人要求我建归档库，但我建了，后来所有人找素材快了 30%。

我不太安分，是安静的那种。比起描述一件很大的完美的事，我更愿意做出一件很小的、有点怪的东西。我用两种语言读书，用三种语言打招呼，拍食物照片像别人集邮。我也比嘴上承认的更在意好不好看——配色、穿搭、页面，其实是同一件事。

这里是网上的一小块地方。灯是亮的。
